What's new

The most recent changes will be listed first. Not every small fix makes the list — just the bits actually worth knowing about.

11 October 2026
  • Sentences rebuilt, on the Japanese app's model: who, when, where, with whom, how and what are mixed fresh each time, with checks so the mix makes sense (no museums at midnight, no flying to Sintra). Thousands of different sentences now: plans and what happened, getting up and going to bed (levanto-me, deitei-me), jobs, describing things (o quarto é pequeno, mas está limpo), the weather, likes and preferences, and two-line exchanges using tu. It's on the greeting card and at the top of the Grammar page.
  • The daily greeting now ends with a sentence - or a short exchange - built fresh each time: routines, plans, what happened yesterday, likes, ordering at a café, asking the way, opening times. Tap a line to hear it; tap ↻ for another.
  • Topics tidied: related lists merged (33 down to 21) and split across three pills - Basics, Travel and Life - instead of one long list. Scores are unaffected.
  • 250 more words in 11 new topics: position & compass, how you feel (tenho fome), everyday expressions (se calhar, pois, pronto), quantities, body & pharmacy, shops, phone & travel kit, countries & nationalities, time expressions, seasons & festivities, and jobs & free time. New Group: Sentence glue.
  • Numbers: random numbers and times to read, new every round. Times come both the everyday way (às duas e meia da tarde, três menos um quarto) and the timetable way (às catorze e trinta), with at / until / from / around. Plus look-ups for any number or time.
  • A daily greeting in Portuguese the first time you open the app each day - greeting, date and time, tap a line to hear it. Tap "Português" at the top to see it again.
  • Say it out loud: a new switch for vocab, verbs and contractions. See the prompt, say the answer, tap Show answer to see and hear it, then mark yourself. Speaking-speed recall, without spelling getting in the way.
  • Verbs: 51 everyday verbs in five tenses - present, past, was / used to, right now (estar a…) and going to (ir…). Pick verbs and tenses, then drill the forms; hold a verb to see its full table (tap a row to hear it).
  • Grammar: ten short rule cards (endings, past tenses, ser or estar, contractions, where me/te/se go, tu/você/o senhor, agreement), most with a drill attached.
  • A contractions drill - no, na, do, ao, pelo… filled into real sentences.
10 October 2026
  • First version: the Japanese drill app's engine with a European Portuguese starter vocabulary — 561 words in 22 topics, with Brazilian forms shown where they differ.
  • Typing is accent-aware: missing accents are accepted with a reminder (or marked wrong, if you prefer — see Settings).
