A simple Portuguese drilling app

Andrew Brittle, 2026

European Portuguese vocabulary, drilled the same way as the Japanese drill app: pick some words, answer four-option questions or type the answers, and the app keeps a confidence score for every word so the weak ones come round more often.

Finding words

Groups are collections that cut across topics: Latest words, Trip essentials (greetings, useful phrases and signs), Eating & drinking out, Getting around town, Core words, and Portugal vs Brazil (every word where Brazil says it differently).

Basics, Travel and Life hold the word lists themselves, split into three so each is a short list. Search looks in the Portuguese, the English and the Brazilian forms, and ignores accents, so nao finds não.

The dice opens the random drills: 20 words from everything, your weakest 20, or 20 you haven't been tested on yet.

Drilling

Tap words to select them, or use Select all / Random 20, then Drill with this selection. Hold a card to hear it and see any notes; with Tap a card to hear it on, the page becomes a talking phrasebook.

Reverse the drill direction
See the English and pick the Portuguese.
Type the answer instead of choosing
Type the Portuguese yourself. On its own it shows you the English; with Listen on, it's dictation — write down what you hear.
Listen instead of reading
Hear the word instead of seeing it. Needs a Portuguese voice on your phone (see below).
Say it out loud
See the English and say the Portuguese out loud. Tap Show answer (or press Space) to see and hear it, then mark yourself: Got it (1) or Not this time (2). Your mark is scored like any other answer. Also on the Verbs and Grammar screens.

How typed answers are marked

  • Capitals, extra spaces and punctuation never matter.
  • Nouns come with their article (o comboio, a estação) because that's how the gender gets learnt. Leaving the article off still counts, with a reminder. The wrong article (a comboio) is marked wrong.
  • Right letters but wrong or missing accents (voce) counts, with a reminder and half the usual confidence boost — unless you turn on Accents must be right when typing in Settings.
  • Where Brazil uses a different word (o trem for o comboio), the Brazilian form counts, with a note giving the Portuguese one.
  • Entries like obrigado / obrigada accept either form.

Getting a Portuguese (Portugal) voice

Listening and tap-to-hear use your phone's own text-to-speech. A Brazilian voice sounds very different from Portugal, so the app looks for a Portuguese (Portugal) voice first. On an iPhone or iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Portuguese (Portugal), and download Joana or Catarina. On Android: the Google text-to-speech settings, language Portuguese (Portugal).

Verbs

Pick one or more tenses at the top, tap the verbs you want (or Select all), then Drill 20 forms. Each question gives a person and a verb - nós · falar - with the tense and the English, and you pick or type the form. Every person-and-tense combination has its own confidence score, so the forms you get wrong come back more often. Hold a verb to see its full table; tap a row there to hear it.

Numbers

Reading times and Reading numbers make up 10 new questions every round. Times come two ways: everyday, as people say them (São três menos vinte da tarde), and timetable, as stations and opening hours give them (às quinze e quarenta). Both use às / até às / a partir das / por volta das, which is the contractions rule in action. The look-up tools under each show how any number or time is said; tap the answer to hear it. These two have one confidence score each rather than one per question.

Grammar

Short rule cards, each with the pattern, the exceptions worth knowing and what Portugal does differently from Brazil. Most end with a drill of just that rule. The contractions drill puts no, na, do, ao, pelo… into real sentences.

At the top of the Grammar page (and on the daily greeting) is a sentence or short exchange, built fresh from the app's people, places, times and verbs, to show the grammar working together. Tap a line to hear it; tap ↻ for another.

Your progress

Everything is stored on this device only. Settings has Download backup / Restore from backup to move your scores to another phone.
